Support – Amis à Pattes

Need help with the Amis à Pattes app? This page gathers answers to the most common questions and explains how to reach us. The simplest way is to write to contact@amisapattes.ci.

Contact us

Write to us at contact@amisapattes.ci. Please include the phone number of your account, your phone model and, if possible, a screenshot of the problem. We reply as soon as we can.

You will also find answers in the app's help center (Profile, then Help) and on https://www.amisapattes.ci.

Sign-in and SMS code

You sign in with your phone number: you receive a code by SMS. If the code does not arrive, check the number you entered and your phone's network, then wait a minute before requesting a new code.

To change your phone number, write to us from your old number or mention it in your message.

Orders and delivery

Add products to your cart, choose or create a delivery address on the map, then confirm. You can follow the order status in your history and print the details as a PDF.

If an order is late, incomplete or damaged, write to us with its number.

Payment

You can pay with Mobile Money (Orange, MTN, Wave) or on delivery. If a payment fails, check your balance and your number, then try again.

If you were charged but the order does not show as confirmed, send us the order number, the amount and the time of payment.

Adoption and appointments

From the adoption catalog, you can request a visit or reserve an animal. Service appointments (vet, grooming, etc.) are booked from their own section. To change or cancel, contact us with the date and the service.

Notifications and location

If you do not receive notifications, check that they are allowed for Amis à Pattes in your phone settings.

Your location is requested only while the app is open, to place an address on the map. You can decline it and move the pin by hand.

Delete my account

To delete your account and data, write to contact@amisapattes.ci from the email address linked to your account, or state the phone number of the account, with the subject "Account deletion". We will confirm once it is done. Some accounting data may be kept where the law requires it.

Personal data

To learn which data the app uses and how to exercise your rights, see our privacy policy.
