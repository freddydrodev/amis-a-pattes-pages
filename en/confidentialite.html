Privacy Policy – Amis à Pattes

Amis à Pattes is an app for animal lovers in Côte d'Ivoire: shop, pet adoption, service appointments and pet health records. This page explains which personal data the app uses, why, who it is shared with and how to exercise your rights. Contact: contact@amisapattes.ci.

Who is responsible for your data

The Amis à Pattes app is published by the Amis à Pattes team (website: https://www.amisapattes.ci). For any question about your data, write to contact@amisapattes.ci.

Data we collect

Phone number: used to create your account and sign you in with a code sent by SMS.

Full name and email address: the name is requested at sign-up. The email address is optional.

Delivery addresses: you place them on a map. They are saved in your account for your orders.

Your pets' profiles and health records: the information you enter about your animals.

Orders, adoption reservations and service appointments: their content and history.

Mobile Money payment details: the number used and the amount, when you pay online. Cash on delivery does not involve this.

Notification identifier: a token specific to your device, needed to send you push notifications.

Location

The app asks for access to your location (precise or approximate) only while it is open, to help you place your delivery address on the map. We do not use your location in the background. You can decline this permission and move the pin on the map by hand.

Why we use this data

We use it to create and secure your account, process and deliver your orders, manage your reservations and appointments, display your pets' profiles, send you useful notifications (order status, appointment reminders) and answer your support requests.

The app shows no advertising and we do not sell your data.

Who it is shared with

We rely on providers that process some data on our behalf: an SMS service for your phone number (sign-in code), Duticotac for Mobile Money payments (number and amount), and Firebase (Google) to deliver push notifications using your device identifier. A map service displays the map background on the address screen.

These providers only receive the data needed for their task. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account is active. When your account is deleted, we erase the data about you, except what we must keep to meet a legal or accounting obligation, such as the history of paid orders.

Your rights and account deletion

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, in line with Ivorian law no. 2013-450 on the protection of personal data.

The app does not yet have a delete button. To delete your account and data, write to contact@amisapattes.ci from the email address linked to your account, or state the phone number of the account. We will confirm once the deletion is done.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you may contact the Côte d'Ivoire telecommunications/ICT regulatory authority (ARTCI).

Security

Exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted. We limit access to data to the people who need it. As no system is perfect, please do not share your SMS codes with anyone.

Children

The app is intended for adults who own or love animals. It is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes to this page

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The version in force is the one published on this page. For any important change, we will let you know in the app.
